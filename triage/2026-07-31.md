## Phase 4c — TOO-BIG (with proposed splits)

**A. Source-vintage semantics** (the approved-but-unwritten plan). Too large as one unit
because it spans schema, gathering, and presentation.
1. Classify every source as `statutory` / `documented-event` / `periodic-survey` — labels
   only, no behaviour change.
2. Add a `url_stability` property (`dated` vs `rolling`) to the registry; the IBM incident
   proves rolling URLs are a real class. Report-only first.
3. Inflation-normalise historical monetary values against an official index, showing the
   nominal figure and the index used.
4. Only then consider whether vintage affects grades.

**B. Citation verification at scale.** Tempting after the IBM find, but the France
false-positive (`3,59` vs `3.59`) shows naive matching misfires on a good record.
1. Build a *reporting* tool that lists candidate mismatches for human review.
2. Measure its false-positive rate on the 52 known-good sources.
3. Only gate on it if that rate is near zero.

**C. Impact evidence beyond the current wall.** Split by source class rather than by shard:
insurance claims (NetDiligence by revenue band, NAIC), official national cost statistics
(StatCan, ASD), and mandatory-disclosure registries (ADR-0005). Each is independently
valuable and independently abandonable.
