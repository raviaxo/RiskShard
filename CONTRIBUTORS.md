# Contributors

Every number in RiskShard traces to a reviewed public source. This page credits
the people who brought those sources and strengthened the library. Recognition is
part of the product: your source becomes part of the trail every future user can
audit.

## How credit works

- Each accepted contribution is listed below with the contributor, what they
  strengthened, and the public source they brought.
- Standout contributions may be featured in a monthly **Shard Spotlight**.
- Attribution also lives in the evidence trail itself (source registry and
  reviewed records), not only here.
